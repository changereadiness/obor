ABOUT OBOR

A focused intelligence utility for Canadian business.

OBOR monitors China’s economic, trade, policy and market developments and identifies the signals with potential relevance to Canadian businesses.

HOW SIGNALS ARE SELECTED

OBOR prioritizes developments according to Canadian exposure, economic magnitude, sector relevance, trade relevance, immediacy and confidence in the underlying information.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

Facts, interpretation and commercial implications are kept distinct. Uncertainty is disclosed rather than converted into false precision.

OBOR is an awareness and intelligence tool, not investment, legal, regulatory or trade advice. Important information should be verified against primary sources.
