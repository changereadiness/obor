China production-input prices mostly declined in early August

OBOR relevance 60/100Confidence 60/100Markets

SUMMARY

China's monitored production-input basket moved broadly lower in the period, with 31 of 50 products declining and 12 increasing. The distribution provides a useful watchpoint for industrial input costs.

CANADIAN RELEVANCE

Canadian manufacturers sourcing industrial materials or energy-linked inputs from China can treat the movement as a procurement watchpoint, while Canadian producers competing with Chinese manufacturers should monitor whether input-cost changes affect Chinese cost competitiveness.

WHAT HAPPENED

  • 31 of 50 monitored products decreased in price.
  • 12 of 50 increased, while 7 were unchanged.
  • Pure Benzene (Petroleum Benzene, Industrial Grade) -7.3%.
  • Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) -3.5%.

The monitored basket shows broad downward price movement rather than a uniform shift. These are market prices for important means of production and do not by themselves establish producer prices, export prices or Canadian landed costs.
