China production-input prices mostly declined in early August

OBOR relevance 60/100Confidence 60/100Markets

SUMMARY

China's monitored production-input basket showed broad downward movement in early August: 31 of 50 tracked products fell in price, while 12 increased and 7 were unchanged. The largest extracted declines included Pure Benzene (Petroleum Benzene, Industrial Grade) at -7.3% and Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) at -3.5%.

CANADIAN RELEVANCE

For Canadian manufacturers and other businesses sourcing industrial materials or energy-linked inputs from China, the broad decline is a potential procurement watchpoint: supplier prices may soften for some inputs, depending on contracts, inventory positions and pass-through. Canadian producers competing with Chinese manufacturers should also watch whether lower input costs improve Chinese cost competitiveness. The release does not establish the effect on Canadian import prices.
