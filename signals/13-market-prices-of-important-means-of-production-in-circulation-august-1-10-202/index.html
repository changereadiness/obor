China production-input prices mostly declined in August 1–10 2026

OBOR relevance 60/100Confidence 60/100Markets

SUMMARY

Price movement was broad but not uniform across the monitored basket: 31 of 50 products fell, 12 rose and 7 were unchanged. The distribution is a useful watchpoint for industrial input-cost conditions in China, not a direct measure of export or Canadian landed prices.

CANADIAN RELEVANCE

Canadian manufacturers and importers sourcing industrial materials or energy-linked inputs from China should watch whether supplier pricing follows the broader movement. The release itself does not establish any change in Canadian import prices or landed costs.

WHAT HAPPENED
