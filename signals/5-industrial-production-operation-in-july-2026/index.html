China industrial output grew 4.5% in July

SUMMARY

China's industrial sector continued to expand in July, with manufacturing growth remaining solid and high-tech manufacturing significantly outperforming the broader sector. For businesses exposed to Chinese industrial supply or demand, the divergence across sectors matters more than the headline rate alone.

CANADIAN RELEVANCE

Canadian exporters, suppliers and competitors should watch where Chinese industrial growth is accelerating, particularly in high-tech manufacturing, because stronger domestic production can affect input demand, competitive pricing and supply-chain opportunities.
