China production-input prices mostly declined in the reported period

OBOR relevance 60/100Confidence 60/100Markets

SUMMARY

Price movement was broad but not uniform across the monitored basket: 27 of 50 products fell, 20 rose and 3 were unchanged. The distribution is a useful watchpoint for industrial input-cost conditions in China, not a direct measure of export or Canadian landed prices.

CANADIAN RELEVANCE

Canadian manufacturers and importers sourcing industrial materials or energy-linked inputs from China should watch whether supplier pricing follows the broader movement. The release itself does not establish any change in Canadian import prices or landed costs.

WHAT HAPPENED

  • 27 of 50 monitored products decreased in price.
  • 20 of 50 increased in price.
  • 3 of 50 were unchanged.
  • Live Hog (Three-way Crossbred) decreased 5.5% from the previous period.
  • Coke (Quasi Primary Metallurgical Coke) decreased 3.1% from the previous period.
  • Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) increased 9.8% from the previous period.
